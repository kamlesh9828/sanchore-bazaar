Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how customers can request cancellation, report an order issue and receive an eligible refund for orders placed through Sanchore Bazaar.

1. Cancelling an order

If you need to cancel, contact customer support as soon as possible and provide your order ID. Cancellation depends on the current order status and may not be possible after the seller has started preparing, packing or dispatching the order. If the app provides a cancellation option for your order, you may use it.

2. Missing, damaged, incorrect or unavailable items

If an item is missing, damaged, incorrect or unavailable, contact support promptly after delivery or after receiving the order update. Share your order ID and a clear description. We may request relevant photographs or other details to review the request and coordinate with the seller.

3. Review and eligibility

Each request is reviewed based on order details, seller confirmation and information provided. Refunds or adjustments are subject to verification, the nature of the issue and applicable consumer law. This policy does not limit any rights available under applicable law.

4. Online payment refunds

Where an online-paid order is approved for a refund, we will initiate it through the payment provider to the original payment method, where supported. The time for the amount to appear depends on the payment provider, bank and payment method.

5. Cash on Delivery orders

For Cash on Delivery orders, any approved refund or adjustment will be coordinated by customer support with the seller and customer. The method and timing will be communicated after the request is reviewed.

6. How to request help

  1. Contact customer support at +91 9828834349.
  2. Provide your order ID and registered mobile number.
  3. Explain whether the request concerns cancellation, a missing item, damage, an incorrect item or another order issue.
  4. Provide supporting details requested by the support team.

7. Payment safety

Do not share your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or password to receive a refund. Sanchore Bazaar support will never ask you to disclose these credentials.
