Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Order cancellation

Customers may request cancellation by contacting support as soon as possible. Cancellation may not be possible after a seller has prepared, packed or dispatched the order. The app may show order-specific cancellation availability.

Unavailable or incorrect items

If an item is unavailable, materially different from the listing, damaged, or missing, contact support with your order ID and relevant details. We will review the request and coordinate with the seller.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved for an online-paid order, it will generally be initiated to the original payment method through the payment provider. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on the provider and the customer's bank or payment app.

Cash on delivery

For cash-on-delivery orders, any approved refund or adjustment will be coordinated with customer support and the seller.

How to request help

Call +91 9828834349 and provide your order ID, registered phone number and a brief description of the issue.

Set final cancellation cut-offs, return eligibility and refund timelines to match your actual business operations before publishing.
