Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy describes how order cancellation and refund requests are handled on Sanchore Bazaar.

Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation while an order is in an eligible early status. Once a shop has progressed with preparation or the order has been handed to delivery, cancellation may no longer be available. The app or website will show whether a cancellation request can be submitted.

Refund requests

For an eligible delivered and paid order, a customer may submit a refund request with the reason and relevant details. Requests are reviewed by the Sanchore Bazaar administration. Approval depends on the order record and the circumstances reported.

Payment reversal

Approved online refunds are processed through the payment provider. The time taken for the amount to appear depends on the provider and the customer's bank or payment instrument. Cash payments are handled through the support team and the relevant shop.
