Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Sanchore Bazaar coordinates local delivery for orders placed with participating shops in supported PIN-code areas.

Service areas

Delivery is available only for PIN codes enabled by the platform administration. An address outside an active service area may not be accepted at checkout.

Delivery method

Depending on the shop and order, delivery may be handled by a Sanchore Bazaar delivery partner or by the shop's own external delivery arrangement. The selected method and available status are shown during order fulfilment.

Delivery time

Any delivery time shown is an estimate, not a guarantee. Shop preparation time, traffic, weather, partner availability and address accuracy can affect delivery.

Customer responsibilities

Provide a complete address, correct PIN code and reachable mobile number. Please be available to receive the order and contact support promptly if the delivery details need correction.

Support

For delivery questions, share your order ID with support@sanchorebazaar.bg5.net or call +91 98288 34349.
