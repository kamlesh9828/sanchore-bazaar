Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how order delivery works through Sanchore Bazaar. Delivery is available only for orders and locations supported by participating sellers and the Service.

1. Delivery coverage

Our marketplace focuses on Sanchore, Rajasthan. Delivery availability depends on the seller, product and active service area. The app checks the delivery address or location during checkout; an order can be placed for delivery only where service is available.

2. Delivery charges

Any delivery charge or other applicable fee will be displayed in the app before you confirm the order. Charges may vary depending on the seller, distance, order or service conditions.

3. Estimated delivery time

Delivery estimates, if available, are shown in the app or communicated by the seller. These are estimates and may change due to product availability, preparation time, weather, traffic, high demand or other circumstances.

4. Customer responsibilities

Provide a complete and accurate delivery address, location details where requested, and a reachable mobile number. Ensure someone is available to receive the order. If the address or phone number is incorrect or the recipient is unavailable, delivery may be delayed or may not be completed.

5. Order tracking and updates

Order status and available delivery updates are shown in the app. For help with a delayed order, contact support and provide your order ID. We will coordinate with the seller or delivery personnel as appropriate.

6. Failed or delayed delivery

If delivery cannot be completed because the address is inaccessible, the recipient cannot be reached, the service area is unavailable or another operational issue occurs, support may contact you to discuss next steps. Any cancellation or refund will be handled under our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

7. Product condition and order issues

Please check your order when it is received. If an item is missing, damaged or incorrect, contact support promptly with your order ID and details so the issue can be reviewed under the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
