Shipping & Delivery Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Service area

Delivery is offered only in the pin codes or localities enabled by Sanchore Bazaar and participating sellers. Serviceability is checked during checkout.

Delivery estimates

Estimated delivery time, where available, is displayed in the app or confirmed by the seller. Estimates may change due to product availability, preparation time, weather, traffic or other operational conditions.

Delivery charges

Any delivery fee or other applicable charge will be shown before the customer places the order.

Address and receipt

Customers should provide a complete and accurate delivery address and a reachable phone number. Please ensure someone is available to receive the order.

Delayed or failed delivery

If an order is delayed or cannot be delivered, contact support with your order ID so we can coordinate with the seller or delivery team.
