About Sanchore Bazaar

Sanchore Bazaar aims to make local shopping more convenient by helping customers discover products offered by local sellers. Product availability, prices, delivery areas and delivery timelines are shown in the app or confirmed by the participating seller.

For Customers

Discover products

Browse products listed by participating local shops.

Place orders

Submit orders through the app, subject to stock and seller confirmation.

Payments & support

Available payment methods and order status are shown during checkout. Contact support for help with an order.

For Sellers

Join the marketplace

Local shopkeepers may register through the app and provide the requested business details.

Manage your shop

Participating sellers can list products, update prices and stock, and manage orders using available app features.

Seller approval

Seller accounts and listings may be reviewed before they become active. Registration does not guarantee approval.
