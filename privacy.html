Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Sanchore Bazaar handles information when customers use its website or app.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email address and mobile number.
  • Delivery address, PIN code and order details.
  • Location only when you grant location permission for address or delivery features.
  • Basic device, diagnostic and advertising information needed to operate and protect the service.

How information is used

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify registration email addresses, process orders and payments, coordinate delivery, provide customer support, prevent fraud and improve the service.

Service providers

We use Firebase/Google services for authentication and data storage, Cloudflare for backend services, ByteLabs for registration OTP email delivery, Paytm for online payments and Google AdMob for advertising where enabled. Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider; Sanchore Bazaar does not ask customers to share card PINs or banking passwords with us.

Sharing and security

Order information is shared only with the relevant shop and delivery partner to fulfil the order, and with service providers where needed to operate the platform. We use access controls and security rules, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can manage device location and notification permissions in your device settings. For account access, correction or deletion requests, contact support@sanchorebazaar.bg5.net.
