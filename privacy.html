Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy describes how Sanchore Bazaar ("we", "our") may handle information when you use our app or marketplace website.

Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, account credentials, order details, seller/shop details, and information you submit to customer support. The app may request device permissions such as location only where needed for a feature.

How information is used

We use information to create and manage accounts, connect customers with sellers, process and deliver orders, provide customer support, prevent fraud, maintain security, and meet legal obligations.

Payments

Online payments, if enabled, are processed through the payment provider displayed at checkout. We do not intend to store full card numbers, UPI PINs, passwords, or other payment authentication secrets. Payment providers handle payment credentials under their own policies.

Sharing

Relevant order information may be shared with the seller and delivery personnel to fulfil an order. Information may also be shared with service providers who support hosting, messaging, payments, security, or legal compliance.

Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and use reasonable safeguards. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of eligible account information, subject to legal and operational requirements.

Children

The marketplace is not intended for children to independently create transactions. A parent or guardian should assist minors where appropriate.
