Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sanchore Bazaar ("we", "us" or "our") handles information when you use the Sanchore Bazaar website or mobile application ("Service"). We operate a local marketplace that helps customers discover products offered by participating shops in Sanchore, Rajasthan.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, mobile number, email address (if provided), and account or login information.
  • Orders: items ordered, seller details, order history, payment status, support requests and transaction references.
  • Address and location: delivery address and, where you allow location permission or enter it manually, location coordinates used to check serviceability and support delivery.
  • Seller information: shop name, contact and business details, product listings, prices, stock and images submitted by sellers.
  • Device and usage information: basic technical, diagnostic and app interaction information needed to operate, secure and improve the Service. Advertising services may process device or advertising identifiers according to their own settings and policies.
  • Support communications: information you choose to provide when contacting us.

2. How information is used

We use information to create and manage accounts; display local products; process and coordinate orders; verify seller accounts; check delivery service areas; communicate order updates; provide support; prevent fraud and misuse; maintain security; and comply with legal obligations.

3. When information is shared

Relevant order, address and contact details may be shared with the seller and assigned delivery personnel so an order can be prepared and delivered. Information may also be processed by technology, hosting, authentication, mapping, image-storage, analytics, advertising and payment service providers. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect users, the Service or our legal rights.

4. Payments

Where online payment is available, payment processing is handled by the provider shown at checkout. Cash on Delivery may also be offered where enabled. We do not ask you to disclose your UPI PIN, OTP, card PIN or payment password. Payment providers process payment credentials under their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Location permission

Location access is used for relevant app functions such as checking delivery availability or supporting delivery. You can manage location permission in your device settings. If you deny permission, some location-dependent features may require manual address or location entry where available.

6. Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, resolve disputes, maintain records and meet legal requirements.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request correction or deletion of eligible personal information, or to ask questions about its use. Certain records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or legitimate operational purposes. Device permissions can be controlled in your device settings.

8. Children

Children should use the Service only with the involvement and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

9. Changes

We may update this policy when our Service or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
